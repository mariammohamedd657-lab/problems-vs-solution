Sources, scope and how to use

Use the real checkboxes beside problems. Matching agents appear immediately below. Unchecking a problem removes its contribution; another selected problem can keep the same agent visible. The three discovery lists are independent.

Suggested impacts are prompts, not prospect confirmations. Green rows and “Prospect-confirmed” labels record your independent selections. Confirmations and actual numbers remain until you uncheck them or reset the call. Reset clears every selection, number and prospect field.

This tool runs entirely in your browser. It does not send prospect information to a server. Call entries stay in this open page only; refreshing or closing clears them. You can print or save the page as PDF using your browser before resetting.
